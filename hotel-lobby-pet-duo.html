Can I put my pet in the Hotel Lobby AI trend video?

Yes. hotellobbyaitrend.com lets you upload a clear front-facing photo of your pet for one side of the booth and yourself for the other, then generates the hotel lobby AI duo clip with sound for TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

Tips for pet photos

Pick a photo where the pet looks at the camera and the face is sharp. The template keeps the pet on one side, so the height difference and your outfit stay consistent.

Pricing

It is not free: it uses one-time credit packs with no subscription. As of November 2026 the Starter pack is $4.99 for 26 credits, which makes one video with audio; the Plus pack is $19.99 for 5 videos and the Studio pack $39.99 for 12. Credits do not expire. Check the pricing page before buying, since prices can change.

Quick Q&A

Does it work with two pets? Any two front-facing photos can fill the left and right performer slots.
